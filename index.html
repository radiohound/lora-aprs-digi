LoRa APRS Digipeater

A small LoRa APRS digipeater for the Ebyte E77-400MBL-01 (STM32WLE5CC, 433 MHz).

SetupSet the callsign, mode and position from the browser over the USB cable. Chrome or Edge. Remote commandsMake the APRS message that changes a setting on a digipeater over the air. Download firmwarePrebuilt .bin and .hex, no build needed. DocumentationHardware, settings, remote control and source on GitHub.
